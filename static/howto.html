How to use

Make the bills

  1. On Data, type each client’s mowing price, hedge price, email, and phone.
  2. Pick the month. Upload photos of the work-completed sheet, review the days, then send them to the datastore. You can also add one row without a photo.
  3. On Billing, choose that month and click Generate PDFs.

Who gets the bill

  • A client with an email address gets the bill by email. If the email field is blank, an address in the notes is used instead.
  • A client with a phone and no email gets the bill by text. Download SMS bills only, or email those PDFs to Nick so he can send the texts. They are not emailed to the client.
  • A client with neither is a paper bill. Download mail PDFs only.

Changing an email on the billing table updates the delivery label right away. Email all bills skips anyone who was already emailed.

While you review a PDF

Open a bill and choose Edit bill.

  • A discount lowers the amount that sales tax is calculated on, and it is not given a visit date. Sales tax stays 6.35%.
  • Add bill total from previous month asks which month and the total from that month. By default that amount is before tax, so this month’s tax covers this month’s work and that total together.
  • Check “This amount already includes sales tax” when the number you type is the grand total from that month. It is added after this month’s tax and is not taxed again.

Sales tax and the total update in the editor as you change lines. Save regenerates the PDF. Delete bill removes that client’s PDF and this month’s work, so Generate will not recreate it.
