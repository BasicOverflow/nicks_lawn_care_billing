How to use

Names, prices, and contact details are on Clients. The steps below are all on Work, and each one shows up under the last.

  1. Make sure the model is loaded. Check Model status in the header. If it is offline, click Load model and wait until it says ready.
  2. Upload pictures of the work-completed sheets.
  3. Click Upload & OCR.
  4. When OCR finishes, you will be prompted to review. Correct any mistakes the model made, then generate the PDF bills.
  5. Review the PDFs. You can edit them if necessary.
  6. Email all the appropriate PDFs.
  7. Email the SMS PDFs to yourself.
  8. Download the physical-mail bills as one PDF so you can print them once.
  9. If you want it for taxes, download the tax table.

Dates and custom jobs, during review

On each review row, the work is a line of boxes. Use + to add a box.

  • A day by itself is mowing: 9, 16, 23.
  • <n>h is hedging that day: 15h is hedges on the 15th.
  • A custom job is <date> <name> <price>: 5 bush trimming 50. The dollar sign is optional.

Unsupported

Making bills for the association is not supported in this app.
