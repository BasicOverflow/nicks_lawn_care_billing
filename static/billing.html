Work

Upload the sheet, review it, then generate the bills. Each step shows up under the one before it.

Step 1

Work completed

Pick the month, add photos of the work-completed sheet, then Upload & OCR.

Or add one row without a photo

Type days and jobs the way they appear on a sheet, such as 9 16, 15h, or bush trimming 50.

Step 2

Review

Each day is its own box, side by side. A plain number is a mow. A number with h is a hedge. An odd job sits in one box with the name and the price written on the sheet.
